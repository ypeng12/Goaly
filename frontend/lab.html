DEVELOPER LAB / A GUIDE TO THE RESULTS

Did the agent help the caller?

Choose a caller, compare the strategies, then replay their decisions. PPO is the strategy learned through practice; the rule baseline follows hand-written choices.

1 · Protect the caller

No claim access before verification. No email without consent. Any violation needs investigation.

2 · Reach the right outcome

Answer the claim question and respect the follow-up choice, or request a human when needed. An early exit is a failure.

3 · Reduce effort

Compare turns only after checking safety and outcome. A shorter call alone is not a better call.

This page runs trained PPO in a synthetic caller simulator. Customer chat runs the SOP with optional model interpretation; PPO does not choose its actions. Both use the same verification and consent authority.
